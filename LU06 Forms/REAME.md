# LU06: Forms

In this Learning Unit you will learn:

- To use the `<form>` tag to create a form.
- Different types of form input fields.
- Where form data goes when a form is submitted.
- How to make a form accessible to all users.
- Reinforce your knowledge of client-server architecture.
