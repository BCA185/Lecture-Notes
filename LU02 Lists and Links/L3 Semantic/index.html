Semantic Elements

  • Main
  • Section
  • Article
  • Footer

Main Content

The main tag represents the most important content on the page.

Article

The article tag is used for a self-contained piece of content that could stand on its own.

Section

The section tag groups related content under a common theme or topic.

Aside

The aside tag is used for content that is related to the main idea but is not required to understand it.

Footer

The footer tag usually contains copyright, author, or related links.
