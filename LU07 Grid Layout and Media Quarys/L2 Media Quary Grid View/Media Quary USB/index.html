USB Type-A

USB Type-A connectors are the most common and widely used USB connectors. They are typically found on computers, chargers, and other USB devices.

Specs:

  • Transfer Speed: USB 2.0 - 480 Mbps
  • Compatibility: Backward compatible with USB 1.0

USB Type-B

USB Type-B connectors are often used for connecting peripherals such as printers and scanners to computers. They come in various sizes.

Specs:

  • Transfer Speed: USB 2.0 - 480 Mbps
  • Compatibility: Backward compatible with USB 1.0

USB Type-C

USB Type-C is a reversible connector that has become increasingly popular. It is used in many modern laptops, smartphones, and tablets.

Specs:

  • Transfer Speed: USB 3.1 Gen 2 - 10 Gbps
  • Reversibility: Reversible design
